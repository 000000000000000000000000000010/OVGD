# Game Summary

Carnaval Caramelo (CC) is a Rhythm platformer in which you play as Mike, a musician against trying to get back to his city, but in its path will encounter many obstacles whom he must conquer in order to be accepted back in its town.

## Game Design Pillars

## Pillar 1 - Music leading

What it is: the game in built around music, it guides the gameplay and level design, the purpose of the game is to recreate the energy of the dance floor

How it works: each song is linked with each level and boss, creating an atmosphere unique to each level

Why it matters: the main focus of the game is to explore different towns thought its corresponding song, so each song is representative of its town, creating the idea of City by playing each song

## Pillar 2 - Player flow

What it is: the beat never have to stop, and the game always is going to offer paths for the player to follow as they see fit

How it works: By encouraging the continuous combo during every level, so the player always have paths to choose

Why it matters: as long as the player is playing the level, the beat never stops, recreating the atmosphere of the dance floor

## Pillar 3 - Exploration

What it is: It's the quality of the game to allow different paths for the player to follow, according to their choices, allowing to continue each path as the see fit, creating different hit combos

How it works: the player can start and finish a path and then decide where to continue next, or to switch paths in order to create a bigger combo or an infinite combo thorough all the level

Why it matters: it allows the player to see to totality of a level, and by extension it’s town, allowing the player to experience each town

  

## User Experiences

Overview of the Player Experience: The experience which we are trying to create is one similar to the dancefloor, but in this case you are the DJ keeping a fluid and constat music beat, making the player and everyone in the room dance

Emotional Journey: The player will start with curiosity and excitement, later on is expected to encounter some challenge, and maybe some frustration, that could be overcome through perseverance, and then excitement again and fulfillment at the end 

Player Engagement: the game will start with minimum exposition, focusing on the short time rewards of fun gameplay loops, but the game will always present a challenge  through progression in complexity in each level, and according to level selection. Also there is a layer of narrative design in each level and encounter, painting a story that spans the whole experience 

Interactive Experience: Our  main character will travel through each level, in a linear manner (left to right) but will have the theedom to move and explore each level vertically, discovering different paths to the end of each level, and finding new story pieces, scattered across each borough 

The control scheme that is presented  accounts for automatic scrolling, so the player will use the control inputs to play each instrument - lane. To this end the player will use the D pad and button inputs of the controller in conjunction in order to execute an instrument - lane. 

Visual wise, every good input will have an positive feedback seen, in world as npc  react to a good combo or precise game play 

Sensory Experience: 

-Sound: The game is based on a playlist gathering different artist from de Future Funk genre, so almost all sounds and music comes from these artist 

-Visuals: Stylized, colorful, and vibrant aesthetics 

Memorable Moments: the story is revealed in a progressive manner. In the beginning the player is mostly invested in the gameplay and exploration, and through them, the player keeps discovering the larger story at play 

  
  
  

## Core Gameplay

Summarize the Core Experience: Rhythmically platforming across different  boroughs, exploring and discovering different paths to reach the end of each borough, and conquering each boos, whom will be the main obstacle in Mike’s path

Define the Game's Objective:Each level is a long  battle against a boss who owns its respective borough, this take the form of a rhythm battle, thought wich Mike can discover each corner of each borough

Highlight the Unique Selling Points (USPs):Gameplay wise, what sets apart Carnaval Caramelo from other stylized platformers or rhythm games is its unique blend of explorative platforming and rithmic battles, which allows for different paths and endings to each level 

Explain the Emotional and Cognitive Engagement: The emotional intent of the game is mainly to feel excitement and joy playing each level. But each encounter and borough describe a piece of the whole story of Caramelo City and why is now in the stage that is

Mention the Target Audience: The game is aimed to casual players whom could enjoy an musical evening and see their home as a place for joy and dance

## Game Design Goals

Identify Core Objectives: The player must conquer each boss in their respective level in order to free the whole city and discovering its musical secrets 

Detail Gameplay Experience: The game is aimed to create the feel of joy and excitement of listening to the music you like to listen in the dancefloor with a little sprinkle of challenge and exploration

Define Key Mechanics: In the game you will be given the choice of choosing different paths, corresponding to each instrument that constitutes the song. By completing each section of the song they will be able to complete the whole song and conquer each boss allowing the player to advance towards the final boss and freeing the  city of its current state.

Focus on Player Engagement: each level will start at a simple stage, slowly adding complexity and different paths to explore through the levels, and different ways of conquer each opponent opening different ways of fighting the final stages of the game and endings

Set Quality Benchmarks: CC aims towards a stable performance, which is key to ensure fluid gameplay. With stylized art and its funky music selection by different artist, we believe that player engagement will be interesting 

  

Incorporate Innovation and Uniqueness: Gameplay wise it's a combination of a fighting game, a platformer and a rhyming game. By being able to create combos which allows the player to access different parts of each level, and different outcomes. Also, the game is configured as an album, so each song is a part of a music selection aimed to recreate the atmosphere of the dancefloor

Ensure Accessibility: The game will have more than one color mode to ensure that everyone can see the different visual cues which could be musically and color based

Consider Longevity and Replayability: By playing only one run of the game it would be impossible to see and explore everything that the game have to offer, so we include several hints in each level to encourage player exploration so they can discover different paths and ways to finish the game

## Example of Design Goals Section

Design Goals

Engaging Narrative Experience: 

Caramelo City: Once a joyful city, is now a shell of itself. Its inhabitants seem trapped in a city that used to be their home, but now seems more like a conjunction of jails. its citizens, commonly known as Caramelians, are now helpless against the tyranny that runs rampant in each corner of their beloved city

Mike: is our main character, he is on a journey back to his city and discovers that has been overrun by a gang of corrupt musicians led by a mysterious leader controlling everything from  the shadows of his studio aided by the cunning Dr. Doppler, a skilled sound engineer with no good intentions.

Is up to mike to see how he conquers this menace, which he can fight against or maybe, maybe convince them of collaboration into a freeing the city into a new apogee

SoundGang members: there are 8 of them, each more vicious than the next. Every single one of them controls a borough and runs it as they see fit, terrorizing its inhabitants and keeping them as their puppets

Innovative Gameplay Mechanics: this is a rhythm platformer so every interaction is a musical one. The way you platform is always having a musical impact, whether on the percussion or other instruments

Other side of player empowerment is through the level selection: the order in which the player approach the song selection may have and impact towards the end of the game, maybe some of the bosses will be especially angry o maybe they help Mike 

  

Emotional and Cognitive Engagement: The main emotion that we are looking to evoke is joy through second to second gameplay. Although Its overall set of emotions is positive and joyful, that doesn't mean that there is no deeper emotional range: Caramelo city is in a complicated state, its citizens aren't really happy in the current situation; the different  members of the SoundGang aren't all on good terms with Dr. Doppler and his boss, creating tension inside the gang itself

The current state of the city seems balanced on a thin rope 

High-Quality Visuals and Sound: Visual wise, we are looking to create an impactful artstyle, with heavy use of cell shaded materials  and graphic textures. We aim for a stylized game which could resist the passage of time.

Sound wise, the game is based on the collaboration of different artists who will collaborate with a song for each level, which is the foundation for the level design and gameplay of each level. Also those songs are selected for its emotion relation with the cities that  inspired every level

Accessible and Inclusive Design: In Caramelo City none has a gender as they born, the choose who and how to be as they grow old, so anyone could be whatever they want

Accessibility wise, we are developing several tools to make this experience an accessible one, with text narratives and sounds that can be  listened to by everyone. Also we create a colorblind mode, and will include several visual aids so everyone can enjoy this experience

Replayability and Longevity: There will be different paths for the player to follow, through stage selection they will decide the way they approach the final encounters and the different orders in which the song selection will play and how they confront  Dr. Doppler and his boss 

We plan to create levels that can be explored in several ways, but never the whole level in just one playthrough. That way we  encourage the player to discover different approaches to each level, so they can discover what each borough offers 

Community and Player Feedback: Carnival Caramelo forsers a community of motivated members, willing to test and provide feedback in gameplay and story. We care very much about their comments and balance feedback, and try to incorporate as much as we can in the game

Balanced Difficulty Curve: the game has a tutorial level, which is the first one that the player has to play, in this level they will learn the basic mechanics of the game on which we will build the secret difficulty curve. This curve will be expressed in the level selection but will be hidden for the player to discover through multiple runs

## Features

General:

Branching story paths and endings: According to player level choice and the way they play each level, the boss encounters would have different outcomes as well the end of the story

Visual responsiveness: Player interaction always will have visual feedback, according to gameplay. Also the environments will respond to player gameplay, allowing for exploration and discovery

Gameplay:

Branching level gameplay:  Each level is constituted by instrument-lanes that represent different instruments 

Combo system: Through platforming the player can create combos, in the same instrument-lane or combine them to create massive combos that could account for sections of each level. This combos could be as creative as  the player wish, so they can change lanes anytime, as long as there is a platform for the player to be 

Constant side scrolling: All levels have autoscrolling, which means the player is always moving forward, and de beats are constant

Fluid Gameplay: The game intent to create a fluid experience, in which the player can always interact with the level in one way or another

## Player Mechanics

Shovel knight / Ducktales jump: (X button) Percussions, according  with each platform shape, Mike will perform different kinds of jumps

Sliding: (Triangle button) When found, rails can be slides, and according to it’s direction and player input, they can move Mike on different notes  

Stair chord: (square button) When climbing a Stair chord, and according to it’s direction and player input, they can move Mike on different notes

Combo: It's a sequence of successful player inputs on a level, these can have different lengths and names, according to its lengths 

Musi-combo: is a special kind of combo, reserved to specific sections of each level, which can ‘preplay’ a section or sections of a level

## User Interface

Overview of the UI: The UI must orbit around the gameplay and must follow its principles (responsiveness, snapines, clearines and character). By having this consistency we hope to deliver an immersive and intuitive experience. All of these decisions were made with extensive user testing, to ensure good response 

UI Elements and Layouts: 

-Home screen: Is the first interactive screen for the player, it serves as the central hub for the other aspects of the game. it must invite and motivate the player to play and never be an obstacle

-Settings: where the player can set different aspects of the game to serve its own necessities among the options provided. We present a series of setting such as resolution, audio quality, different aesthetic filters and colorblinds filters) 

-Overworld: is the level selection screen. After the game is set up for the player to enjoy, is moment for them to start the path of choosing which way to go 

-In Level: the game offers a HUD that includes the best combos made in level, level of precision that the player must have a minimum amount 

-Pause: This overlay allows the player access the possibility to change some settings of  the game, and being able to quit the level, to go to the overworld or to quit the game entirely
