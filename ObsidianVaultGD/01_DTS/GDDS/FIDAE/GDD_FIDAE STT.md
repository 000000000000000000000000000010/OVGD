# 00_Context

## Context
Este simulador está orientado a capacitar de forma inmersiva a los usuarios en el manejo seguro y responsable de armas de fuego, acercándolos a procedimientos reales como disparos a diferentes tipos de dianas, resolución de fallas frecuentes y protocolos de seguridad y emergencia propios de un polígono de tiro. El proyecto busca entregar una experiencia práctica, realista y educativa que permita comprender profundamente la operatividad de las armas y fomentar conductas adecuadas frente a situaciones reales.

La experiencia sumerge al usuario en un entorno virtual cuidadosamente diseñado para replicar un polígono de tiro profesional. El objetivo es acercar y capacitar a los participantes, familiarizándolos con los protocolos de seguridad, los procedimientos operativos y el manejo adecuado de armas de fuego, todo en un ambiente controlado.


## Training Objectives
El usuario podrá iniciar una práctica guiada o no guiada, donde podrá generar un puntaje tanto por precisión como por ejecución correcta de los procedimientos. Al finalizar cada experiencia, se entregará un informe de desempeño al instructor, permitiendo una evaluación personalizada y la generación de un ranking basado en los resultados obtenidos.

-Simulación de zonas de tiro en entornos controlados.
-Simulación de operativos en escenarios realistas.
-Instructivos interactivos sobre el uso seguro de armas en polígonos de tiro.
-Protocolos guiados para el comportamiento y manejo responsable de armas de fuego.


## Target User
Este simulador está dirigido exclusivamente al sector profesional (B2B), especialmente a instituciones públicas o privadas que requieren capacitar a su personal en el uso responsable y seguro de armas de fuego. Está diseñado para ser utilizado por cuerpos de seguridad, fuerzas armadas, academias de formación, y centros de entrenamiento táctico, proporcionando una herramienta formativa que refuerza tanto la precisión técnica como el cumplimiento de protocolos de seguridad.
## Learning Pillars
https://www-unesco-org.translate.goog/en/dtc-financing-toolkit/six-pillars?_x_tr_sl=en&_x_tr_tl=es&_x_tr_hl=es&_x_tr_pto=tc
**Data and evidence**
The data and evidence pillar focuses on using data to inform and improve educational experiences. It emphasises the importance of generating practical recommendations through data analysis, ensuring interconnected and interoperable data systems, applying standardised methods for accurate and comprehensive data collection, establishing strong data protection measures, and ethically leveraging AI to enhance education processes.

Central to this pillar is the bidirectional relationship between data and all other aspects of digital transformation. Data shapes decisions across areas like capacity development, content, and infrastructure—while digital transformation, in turn, produces richer, more useful data that feeds back into planning and improvement.

Without robust data and evidence systems, digital transformation efforts risk lacking direction, impact, and the ability to drive continuous improvement in education.


**Connectivity and infrastructure**
Connectivity and infrastructure focuses on the availability, reliability, and accessibility of the technical systems needed to support digital education. It includes five key components: electricity; internet access; hardware and devices; system software and services; and learning spaces.

This pillar emphasises the importance of reliable electricity and affordable internet for all educational institutions, adequate access to digital devices and technical systems, strong IT support and security measures, and adaptable physical spaces that enable effective digital learning.

It also considers online–offline alternatives to support learning in areas with limited connectivity, including mobile and low-tech solutions. In addition, it recognises the need for renewable energy infrastructure to improve sustainability, and the importance of secure digital environments, supported by system software and services that protect sensitive educational data.

**Content and solutions**
Content and solutions examines the quality, accessibility, and relevance of digital learning materials, tools, and platforms. It focuses on ensuring that educational content is high-quality, open, inclusive, and aligned with curricular standards, and that platforms are secure, user-friendly, and regularly updated.

It includes five key components: learning platforms that effectively deliver and manage content; application software that is high-quality and open to promote equitable access; alignment with curricula to ensure relevance; digital and blended pedagogical models that enhance teaching and learning; and assessment and certification systems that recognise and validate learning.

This pillar emphasises the importance of developing user-friendly platforms that support personalised learning, deploying high-quality digital content aligned with national curricula, adopting innovative pedagogical approaches, and implementing assessment systems that guide learners through structured educational pathways.

## Core Pillars

The core mechanic of a game is the activity a player most frequently does in a game.
The secondary mechanics involve the interactions that are less frequent in the game.
The progression layer involves the way levels are built up and progression is communicated.
The story layer puts everything you are doing in the game into context.
