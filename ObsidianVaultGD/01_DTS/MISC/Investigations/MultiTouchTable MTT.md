Which software is compatible with your hardware?

You can develop your own application or contact [our partners](https://www.interactive-scape.com/partner-network), who will do this for you. Thanks to our robust hardware solutions and the revolutionary [Scape X® Engine](https://www.interactive-scape.com/products/scape-x-engine) with open access to TUIO, we enable anyone to develop multi-touch applications with object recognition. All you need is a software framework that can communicate via the standardised **open-source TUIO protocol** (e.g. Unity, Ventuz, smartPerform, vvvv, Intuiface, Nuiteq Snowflake and many more). Integration is straightforward thanks to ready-to-use TUIO libraries in JavaScript, C++, C#, Python and Java.

If you want to learn more have a look at [http://tuio.org/?software](http://tuio.org/?software).

Is the PC already integrated in the Scape® Tangible Displays and various tables or do I need an external PC?

Our [Scape® Tangible](https://www.interactive-scape.com/products/scape-tangible) **displays** do not have a built-in application PC. The advantage for you is that you are free to choose a PC that meets your specific requirements and can upgrade it as and when necessary, without the display itself being a factor. The displays contain a processing unit running the firmware that enables touch and object recognition.

However, applications require an external computer, which must be connected to the display via a video output, USB (USB Touch) or Ethernet (TUIO) cable. We can supply this PC pre-configured, but this is not essential – you can also use your own PC or laptop.

With our **tables**, it depends: the [Scape® Lab](https://www.interactive-scape.com/products/scape-lab) is supplied without a PC as standard, as it is often purchased by universities, institutes, etc., which wish to use the same table across several departments and therefore with different PCs. With [Scape® Pro](https://www.interactive-scape.com/products/scape-pro) and [Movable](https://www.interactive-scape.com/products/scape-movable), the PC is an integral part of the overall design and features bespoke storage compartments for our PC models, as well as appropriate cabling, ventilation, etc. In this case, it is **not advisable** to use your own PC – although this is technically possible; please [contact us](mailto:sales@interactive-scape.com) to clarify any uncertainties in this regard.
